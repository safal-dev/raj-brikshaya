About the Place

Welcome to Raj Briksha

Built less around a fixed list of amenities, and more around space, time, and food worth talking about.

Raj Briksha sits in front of a wide, quiet pond, with an open stretch of ground running alongside it. It's the kind of setting that does a lot of the work on its own — guests arrive, see the water and the open sky, and start slowing down before they've even checked in.

The resort also sits close to a traditional, indigenous community that has lived on this land long before it did. That closeness gives the whole area a character of its own — one we're still learning to share well, rather than turn into a packaged activity.

The Area Around Us

Close to a Traditional Community

Raj Briksha sits near a community that has followed its own traditional, indigenous way of life here long before the resort existed. That history gives the whole area a character that goes beyond the resort's own walls.

We're still learning the right way to share this with guests — rather than turning it into a packaged activity, we'd rather you experience the area honestly, a little at a time, as we get it right.

Who's Behind It

A Note on the Founder

Raj Briksha Resort was founded by Jeevan Sapkota, built around a simple idea — that a resort doesn't have to repeat what every other resort is already doing. His focus from the start has been on giving the kitchen room to experiment, keeping the grounds open and unhurried, and creating a place guests actually want to slow down in.

Jeevan Sapkota
Founder, Raj Briksha Resort
