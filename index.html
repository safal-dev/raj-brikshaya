Our Story

Welcome to Rajbriksha

A resort grown slowly, the way a golden tree grows — rooted deep, and built to last.

Rajbriksha takes its name from the golden shower tree, Nepal's national flower, whose blossoms mark the turn of the season across the plains. We borrowed the name because it says exactly what this place is: something that takes its time, draws from the land around it, and blooms a little more vivid every year.

Every arch, beam and tiled roof here was raised with that same patience — local timber, local masons, and a design that lets the Terai light pour straight through the windows. There is no rush at Rajbriksha. Just long verandas, open pavilions, and a stretch of green fields that goes on as far as you can see.

Life at Rajbriksha

More Than a Stay — A Shared Table

Rajbriksha was raised by the hands of the village around it, and it's run the same way. From the carpenters who built the sofas to the cooks in the pavilion kitchen, this is a resort that keeps its roots local — and its door open.

Guests often find themselves pulled into the middle of it: a gathering on the lawn, a conversation over tea in the dining hall, a welcome that doesn't feel staged. That's not an experience we package — it's just how things are here.
